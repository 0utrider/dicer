# Sound credits

**Halfwits & Failed Crits** (recorded by Jonathan Swenson on a Neumann TLM103), released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain). Credit is not required but is gladly given; thank you!

- Source: https://halfwitsfailedcrits.itch.io/sfx-pack-rolling-dice
- Support the creators: https://www.ko-fi.com/halfwitsfailedcrits · https://www.HalfwitPodcasts.com

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-dice-sprite.mjs "<unzipped pack folder>"` (needs ffmpeg).
