# Sound credits

`dice-hits.mp3` / `dice-hits.json` are individual dice hits cut from **"SFX Pack – Rolling Dice"** by
**Halfwits & Failed Crits** (recorded by Jonathan Swenson on a Neumann TLM103), released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain). Credit is not required but is gladly given; thank you!

- Source: https://halfwitsfailedcrits.itch.io/sfx-pack-rolling-dice
- Support the creators: https://www.ko-fi.com/halfwitsfailedcrits · https://www.HalfwitPodcasts.com

`dice-hits-felt.mp3` / `dice-hits-felt.json` are dice hits cut from the die and dice throws in
**"Casino Audio" (1.1)** by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), released under **CC0 1.0**; they were recorded on
a felt table and play when the table surface is set to felt.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio

Rebuild with `node scripts/build-dice-sprite.mjs "<unzipped Halfwits pack folder>" "<Kenney Casino Audio/Audio folder>"`
(needs ffmpeg).
